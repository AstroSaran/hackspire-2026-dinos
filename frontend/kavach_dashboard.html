Pilot / representative mode: current scores are generated from representative data and are not field-validated predictions. This is an intentional research boundary — see the Validation Status panel and README.

Villages

Kavach does not replace Meghdoot, e-NAM, PMFBY or MGNREGA. It sits above them as a decision-support layer: it detects convergence across climate, crop, market, water and employment signals, shows why risk is rising, explores plausible trajectories, and helps officials decide what preventive action to review — it does not allocate benefits or make eligibility decisions on its own.
